**Billing, provisioning and AI agents for hosting and cloud providers.**

English · [简体中文](https://github.com/rackmoon/.github/blob/main/profile/README.zh-CN.md)

</div>

RackMoon is a self-hosted business system for hosting and cloud providers. Billing, provisioning, a client area and support tickets live in one place. The core will be free, and we plan to open-source it.

- **One billing engine** for VPS, dedicated servers, GPUs, containers, game servers and domains.
- **Pluggable provisioning.** New product types connect without touching billing code.
- **Supply network.** Resell other providers' products with automatic provisioning.
- **Safe AI agents.** Every action is read-only, confirmed or approved, and logged.

> Early design. We are building in the open, and nothing is ready for production yet.

Questions and ideas go to [issues](https://github.com/rackmoon/.github/issues). Report security problems through the [security policy](https://github.com/rackmoon/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
